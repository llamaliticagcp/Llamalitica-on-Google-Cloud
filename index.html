Company Overview

Llamalitica offers a comprehensive solution for healthcare with customizable modules that enable advanced analysis of clinical data through artificial intelligence.

This platform is capable of extracting information from a wide variety of formats in electronic medical records, including documents, PDFs, images, video, audio, and text.

Key features include generative AI, natural language processing, advanced search, alert systems, cohort selection, population health dashboards, deep phenotyping, and tools for clinical research.

Developed and validated in real healthcare environments, Llamalitica stands out for its flexibility, adapting to any area of the healthcare system that relies on data analysis.

Solution Architecture

Llamalitica on Google Cloud leverages Google Cloud's AI and data analytics services to deliver a scalable, secure, and interoperable healthcare AI platform.

Google Cloud Services Used:

  • ✅ Vertex AI - Training and deploying custom ML models for clinical NLP
  • ✅ Healthcare API - FHIR support for EHR integration
  • ✅ BigQuery - Population health analytics and insights
  • ✅ Cloud Storage - Secure management of multimodal medical data
  • ✅ Google Workspace - Clinical workflow integration
  • ✅ Cloud Run / Kubernetes - Scalable deployment

This integration delivers a scalable, secure, and intelligent platform that enhances clinical decision-making, automates administrative tasks, and accelerates medical research.
